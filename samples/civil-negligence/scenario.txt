FICTIONAL SOFTWARE TEST - Ellery v. Tamarack Quay Holdings Inc. and Greyvale Grounds Ltd.
Jordan Ellery alleges a slip and fall on ice at the east entrance of Tamarack Quay Plaza in Port Alder on January 14, 2025, and sues the plaza owner, Tamarack Quay Holdings Inc., as occupier and its snow contractor, Greyvale Grounds Ltd., for negligence in salting. Tamarack denies breach, says it reasonably relied on an independent contractor, alleges contributory negligence, and crossclaims against Greyvale for contribution and indemnity. Greyvale says it salted the walkways and says the Plaintiff did not give timely written notice. Greyvale has delivered a jury notice. The sources disagree about the time and place of the fall, footwear, phone use, salting, earlier knee problems, income, notice, future surgery and capacity to work; some records are missing. Treat this as one contested action, and do not treat any assertion as established. Ontario, Canada. All names and facts are invented.
