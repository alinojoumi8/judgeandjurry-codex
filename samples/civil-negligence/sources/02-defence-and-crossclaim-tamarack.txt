FICTIONAL TEST SOURCE: Statement of Defence and Crossclaim of Tamarack Quay Holdings Inc.
Ontario Superior Court of Justice. Court file number CV-25-00000-FICT.
Dated December 8, 2025, by Whitcombe Ames LLP, lawyers for the Defendant Tamarack Quay Holdings Inc. ("Tamarack").
DEFENCE
1. Tamarack denies that it breached any duty owed to the Plaintiff under the Occupiers' Liability Act and denies the allegations in paragraphs 9 and 12 of the Statement of Claim.
2. Tamarack reasonably entrusted snow and ice control at the Plaza to an independent contractor, Greyvale Grounds Ltd.
3. If the Plaintiff fell as alleged, which is not admitted, the Plaintiff's own negligence caused or contributed to the fall, and Tamarack relies on the Negligence Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. N.1.
4. Particulars of contributory negligence: the Plaintiff wore footwear that was unsuitable for winter conditions.
5. Particulars of contributory negligence: the Plaintiff was using a phone while walking.
6. The Plaintiff had a pre-existing left knee condition, and any loss attributable to that condition is not recoverable from Tamarack.
7. The Plaintiff has failed to take reasonable steps to mitigate the alleged loss.
CROSSCLAIM
8. Tamarack crossclaims against Greyvale for contribution and indemnity under the Negligence Act and under clause 9.2 of the Winter Services Agreement dated October 1, 2024.
9. Tamarack asks that the claim against it be dismissed with costs.
