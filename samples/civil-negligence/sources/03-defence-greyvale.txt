FICTIONAL TEST SOURCE: Statement of Defence and Defence to Crossclaim of Greyvale Grounds Ltd.
Ontario Superior Court of Justice. Court file number CV-25-00000-FICT.
Dated January 12, 2026, by Sorrel Pike LLP, lawyers for the Defendant Greyvale Grounds Ltd. ("Greyvale").
DEFENCE
1. Greyvale applied salt to the lot and all walkways at 05:30 on January 14, 2025.
2. Greyvale denies that it was negligent and denies that any act or omission of Greyvale caused the Plaintiff's alleged injuries.
3. Greyvale pleads that the Plaintiff did not give written notice of the claim within 60 days as required by section 6.1 of the Occupiers' Liability Act.
4. If the Plaintiff fell as alleged, which is not admitted, the Plaintiff's own negligence caused or contributed to the fall, and Greyvale relies on the Negligence Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. N.1.
DEFENCE TO CROSSCLAIM
5. Under the Winter Services Agreement, Tamarack was responsible for calling out Greyvale for any freezing rain event with less than 2 cm of accumulation.
6. Greyvale denies that it owes contribution or indemnity to Tamarack under the Negligence Act or under clause 9.2 of the Winter Services Agreement.
7. Greyvale asks that the claim and the crossclaim be dismissed with costs.
