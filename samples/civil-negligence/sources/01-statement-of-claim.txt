FICTIONAL TEST SOURCE: Statement of Claim, Ellery v. Tamarack Quay Holdings Inc. and Greyvale Grounds Ltd.
Ontario Superior Court of Justice. Court file number CV-25-00000-FICT. Place of trial: Port Alder.
Issued November 3, 2025, by Hollis Varga LLP, lawyers for the Plaintiff, Jordan Ellery.
CLAIM
1. The Plaintiff claims general damages in the amount of $500,000.
2. The Plaintiff claims special damages and past and future income loss in the amount of $1,000,000.
3. The Plaintiff claims punitive damages in the amount of $50,000.
4. The Plaintiff claims pre-judgment and post-judgment interest, and the costs of this action.
THE PARTIES
5. The Plaintiff is a shift supervisor employed by Kestrel Cold Storage Ltd. and lives in Port Alder, Ontario.
6. The Defendant Tamarack Quay Holdings Inc. ("Tamarack") owns and is the occupier of Tamarack Quay Plaza, 410 Quayside Road, Port Alder, Ontario (the "Plaza").
7. The Defendant Greyvale Grounds Ltd. ("Greyvale") is the contractor that provided snow and ice control services at the Plaza.
THE FALL
8. On January 14, 2025, at approximately 7:45 a.m., the Plaintiff slipped and fell on ice on the walkway at the east entrance of the Plaza.
LIABILITY
9. Tamarack breached its duty as occupier under the Occupiers' Liability Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. O.2, by failing to take reasonable care that persons entering the Plaza were reasonably safe.
10. Greyvale was negligent in failing to salt the walkway at the east entrance before the Plaintiff arrived.
11. The Plaintiff gave written notice of the claim within 60 days after the occurrence, as required by section 6.1 of the Occupiers' Liability Act.
INJURIES AND LOSS
12. As a result of the fall the Plaintiff suffered a left tibial plateau fracture, required surgery, and has chronic pain and a loss of the amenities of life.
13. At the time of the fall the Plaintiff was earning an annual income of approximately $84,000.
14. The Plaintiff proposes that this action be tried at Port Alder.
