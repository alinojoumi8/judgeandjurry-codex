FICTIONAL SOFTWARE TEST — R. v. Alex Morgan
Count 1: The Crown alleges that Alex Morgan dishonestly diverted a $20,000 customer deposit from the Northstar Workshop equipment order to unrelated expenses. Morgan disputes dishonest intent and says the customer authorized temporary operating use pending delivery. The equipment was not delivered and no refund was made before the complaint. Source records show the payment, a later transfer, and an ambiguous email; they do not resolve what Morgan intended when the deposit was accepted. The Crown seeks a finding of guilt; defence counsel seeks acquittal on reasonable doubt. Treat this as one contested count, not multiple charges. Ontario, Canada. All names and facts are invented.
