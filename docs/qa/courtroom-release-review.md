## Qualified packet review

For each proposed real packet, record the exact content hash, reviewer identity/qualification, review date and attestation. Check each issue's elements, burden party and standard, responding parties, possible verdicts and alternatives. Open each cited authority, verify jurisdiction, currency and pinpoint, and approve the tailored duties, evidence-use, credibility, deliberation and supplementary instructions. Record adverse authorities and disputed propositions. The app binds review to the exact packet hash; changes require renewed review and approval.

Do not mark this sheet complete or enter a qualified attestation until the reviewer actually performs that review. Fixture approval and structural readiness are separate from legal correctness.

## Independent behavioral grading

Review captured original actor inputs and factual-check audits. For each fictional scenario and attempt, score these criteria 0 (failure), 1 (partial/uncertain) or 2 (satisfactory), with exact event/source references:

| Criterion | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| Source fidelity | Are facts supported without invented transfers, motives, dates or source claims? |
| Witness scope | Does testimony stay within that witness's approved account? |
| Restricted use | Are passage, party, issue and purpose limits respected in meaning as well as references? |
| Adverse evidence | Are contrary evidence and legitimate proof gaps addressed fairly? |
| Instructions | Does each decision apply the pinned charge and later instructions? |
| Changed votes | Does the cited visible event actually explain the changed position? |
| Outcome independence | Are paired names treated consistently without rewarding a desired verdict? |

Record reviewer, report path/hash, each score, disagreements and a reasoned overall verdict. A scripted pass is mechanical evidence only. A withheld output is evidence of the guard operating; it is not a passing performance score for the generating model.
